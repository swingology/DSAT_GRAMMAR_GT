# Rhetorical Synthesis

> This is a **Digital SAT-specific** question type. You're given bullet points of data and asked to write or choose a sentence that achieves a **specific goal** stated in the prompt.

---

## What It Looks Like

The question will present something like:

> A student is writing a report about renewable energy. The student wants to **emphasize the contrast** between solar and wind energy costs.
>
> **Notes:**
> - Solar panel costs have decreased 89% since 2010
> - Wind turbine installation costs have remained relatively stable
> - Both are cheaper than coal in most markets
> - Government subsidies vary by region

Then you choose from four answer options, each a differently constructed sentence.

---

## The Strategy

### Step 1: Read the Goal First

Before even looking at the bullet points, **read the goal carefully**. Underline the key instruction:

Common goal types:
- **Emphasize a contrast** between X and Y
- **Introduce the topic** to an unfamiliar audience
- **Support a claim** with specific evidence
- **Provide a counterargument** to a position
- **Summarize the main finding** of a study
- **Connect two ideas** / transition between paragraphs

### Step 2: Identify What the Goal Requires

| Goal Keyword | What the Correct Answer Must Do |
|---|---|
| **Contrast** | Mention BOTH things and highlight the difference |
| **Emphasize** | Stress the importance or significance of something |
| **Introduce** | Be broad/accessible, not deep in technical detail |
| **Support with evidence** | Include specific data (numbers, statistics) |
| **Counterargument** | Present the opposing perspective |
| **Summarize** | Capture the main point without extra detail |

### Step 3: Eliminate Answers That Don't Match the Goal

Most wrong answers will:
- Use the correct data but for the **wrong purpose** (e.g., summarizing when the goal is to contrast)
- Only mention **one** of two things that need to be compared
- Include **irrelevant** bullet points that don't serve the stated goal
- Be **true** based on the notes but not responsive to what the question asks

## Example Walkthrough

**Goal:** The student wants to **emphasize the contrast** between the cost trends of solar and wind energy.

A) "Both solar and wind energy are now cheaper than coal in most markets, making renewables an attractive option."
→ ❌ Discusses both but emphasizes **similarity**, not contrast.

B) "Solar panel costs have plummeted 89% since 2010, while wind turbine installation costs have remained relatively stable."
→ ✅ Mentions **both** and highlights the **difference** in cost trends.

C) "Government subsidies for renewable energy vary significantly by region, affecting adoption rates."
→ ❌ Completely ignores the solar vs. wind comparison.

D) "The dramatic 89% decrease in solar panel costs since 2010 has made solar energy increasingly accessible."
→ ❌ Only mentions solar — no contrast with wind.

**Answer: B** — It's the only choice that fulfills the specific goal of contrasting two things.

---

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It's Wrong |
|---|---|
| Picking the answer that uses the most data | More data ≠ better if it doesn't serve the goal |
| Picking the answer that sounds most "academic" | Tone isn't what's being tested — logic and purpose are |
| Getting distracted by true-but-irrelevant information | All bullet points are true; not all serve the goal |
| Confusing "summarize" with "support with evidence" | Summaries are broad; evidence is specific |

**💡 Learning Hint:** Think of it like this — you're a newspaper editor giving instructions to a writer. The "goal" is your instruction. Only one answer choice is what a good writer would produce given that instruction. The others are good sentences that answer a **different** question.

## Practice Question

**Goal:** The student wants to **introduce the topic of the Morpho butterfly's coloring to readers unfamiliar with the species**.

**Notes:**
- The Morpho butterfly is found in Central and South American rainforests
- Its wings appear vivid blue due to light diffraction, not pigment
- The nanostructures on its wings have inspired developments in materials science
- Morpho butterflies have a wingspan of approximately 5–8 inches

A) "The nanostructures on the Morpho butterfly's wings, which cause light diffraction rather than relying on pigment, have led to breakthroughs in biomimetic materials science."
B) "Found in Central and South American rainforests, the Morpho butterfly is known for its vivid blue wings — a color produced not by pigment but by the diffraction of light."
C) "With a wingspan of 5 to 8 inches, the Morpho butterfly's light-diffracting wing nanostructures have inspired innovations in materials science."
D) "Light diffraction, rather than pigment, is responsible for the Morpho butterfly's blue coloring, a phenomenon that researchers have studied extensively."

**Correct Answer: B**
*Why?* The goal is to **introduce** the butterfly to someone who doesn't know about it. B gives accessible context (where it lives, what it looks like, what makes it special) without diving into technical applications. A and C jump into materials science (too advanced for an introduction). D focuses narrowly on the mechanism without establishing basic context.

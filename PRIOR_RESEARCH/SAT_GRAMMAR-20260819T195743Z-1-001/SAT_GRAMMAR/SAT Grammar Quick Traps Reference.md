# SAT Grammar Quick Traps Reference

> Use this as a **rapid review sheet** before practice tests. Each trap links to its full explanation.

---

## Agreement & Reference Traps

| Trap | What to Watch For | Fix | Full Topic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prepositional phrase between subject & verb | "The list of items **are/is**..." | Find the real subject → **is** | [[Subject-Verb Agreement]] |
| Inverted sentence order | "Among the ruins **stand/stands**..." | Flip the sentence to find the subject | [[Subject-Verb Agreement]] |
| Singular indefinite + "their" | "Everyone brought **their**..." | Change to "his or her" or restructure | [[Pronouns]] |
| Ambiguous "it" or "they" | Pronoun with 2+ possible antecedents | Replace pronoun with the specific noun | [[Pronouns]] |
| "I" vs. "me" in compounds | "between you and **I/me**" | Remove the other person to test | [[Pronouns]] |
| Who vs. whom | "the person **who/whom** I called" | Him → whom, He → who | [[Pronouns]] |
| Irregular plural possessive | "childrens'" or "womens'" | Irregular plurals get **'s** (children's, women's) | [[Possessive Nouns and Apostrophes]] |
| Apostrophe on simple plural | "The dog's ran..." | No possession = no apostrophe | [[Possessive Nouns and Apostrophes]] |
| Its vs. it's vs. its' | "The dog wagged **it's** tail" | **its** = possessive, **it's** = it is, **its'** = not a word | [[Possessive Nouns and Apostrophes]] |

## Modifier & Word Form Traps

| Trap | What to Watch For | Fix | Full Topic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adjective vs. adverb after action verb | "She sang **beautiful/beautifully**" | Action verb → adverb | [[Adverbs]] |
| Linking verb + adverb | "She looks **beautifully/beautiful**" | Linking verb → adjective | [[Linking Verbs vs Action Verbs]] |
| Good vs. well | "She did **good/well** on the test" | "Good" = adjective, "well" = adverb | [[Adverbs]] |
| Hard vs. hardly | "He studied **hardly**" | "Hard" = with effort, "hardly" = barely | [[Adverbs]] |
| Fewer vs. less | "**Less** students attended" | Countable → fewer, uncountable → less | [[Adverbs]] |
| Misplaced "only/almost/nearly" | "She almost found every error" | Place adverb next to what it modifies | [[Adverbs]] |
| Dangling modifier | "-ing phrase, [wrong subject]..." | Subject after comma must do the -ing action | [[Participles]] |
| Misplaced participial phrase | "...findings to the board wearing a lab coat" | Move modifier next to what it describes | [[Participles]] |
| -ing vs. -ed participle | "The students were **boring/bored**" | Cause = -ing, Receiver = -ed | [[Participles]] |
| Participle as fragment | "The committee reviewing the data." | -ing alone ≠ main verb; needs helper or rewrite | [[Participles]] |
| Wrong irregular past participle | "She had **went/gone**" | Memorize irregular forms (went → gone) | [[Participles]] |
| Wrong idiomatic preposition | "different **than/from**" | Memorize the correct pairings | [[Prepositions and Prepositional Verbs]] |

## Verb Form & Consistency Traps

| Trap | What to Watch For | Fix | Full Topic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unnecessary tense shift | Past tense paragraph + random present verb | Match the established tense | [[Verb Tense and Mood]] |
| Missing past perfect | "By the time we arrived, the concert **started**" | Earlier of two past events → "had started" | [[Verb Tense and Mood]] |
| "If I was" (hypothetical) | "If I **was** the captain, I would..." | Hypothetical → subjunctive "were" | [[Verb Tense and Mood]] |
| Wrong subjunctive after demands | "She insists he **submits**..." | After demand/recommend verbs → base form "submit" | [[Verb Tense and Mood]] |
| Would/will inconsistency | Mixing "would" and "will" in same context | Pick one and stay consistent | [[Verb Tense and Mood]] |
| Gerund-only verb + infinitive | "She avoids **to make** mistakes" | "Avoid" takes gerund → "avoids making" | [[Gerunds and Infinitives]] |
| Infinitive-only verb + gerund | "They decided **staying**" | "Decide" takes infinitive → "decided to stay" | [[Gerunds and Infinitives]] |
| Hybrid form | "began **to contributing**" | Pick one: "began contributing" or "began to contribute" | [[Gerunds and Infinitives]] |
| Preposition "to" + infinitive | "look forward to **see** you" | "To" here is a preposition → gerund: "to seeing" | [[Gerunds and Infinitives]] |
| Non-parallel list | "hiking, swimming, and **to run**" | All items must match: "hiking, swimming, running" | [[Parallelism]] |
| Non-parallel comparison | "Running is harder than **to swim**" | Both sides must match: "Running...than swimming" | [[Parallelism]] |
| Broken correlative pair | "not only reduces...but also **it improves**" | Structure after each half must match | [[Parallelism]] |

## Sentence Structure & Punctuation Traps

| Trap | What to Watch For | Fix | Full Topic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Comma splice | "IC**,** IC" (only a comma between two sentences) | Add period, semicolon, or FANBOYS | [[Sentence Boundaries and Clause Joining]] |
| Run-on sentence | Two ICs with no punctuation | Add proper punctuation or conjunction | [[Sentence Boundaries and Clause Joining]] |
| Fragment (DC alone) | "Although she tried hard." | Attach to an independent clause | [[Sentence Boundaries and Clause Joining]] |
| Conjunctive adverb comma splice | "IC**, however,** IC" | "However" is NOT a FANBOYS → needs semicolon: "IC**; however,** IC" | [[Transitions and Logical Flow]] |
| Conjunctive adverb interrupter | "however" mid-sentence with commas | Remove the word — if the sentence is still ONE complete thought, commas are correct | [[Transitions and Logical Flow]] |
| "but" vs. "however" swap | SAT swaps FANBOYS for conjunctive adverb | "but" uses comma; "however" uses semicolon — different rules! | [[Transitions and Logical Flow]] |
| Colon after incomplete clause | "His goals were: to win..." | Left side of colon must be a complete IC | [[Colons Semicolons and Dashes]] |
| Semicolon before a list | "She needed items; a pen, paper..." | Use a colon for lists, not a semicolon | [[Colons Semicolons and Dashes]] |
| Mismatched punctuation pairs | "The scientist—who won a prize, lectured" | Dash opens → dash must close | [[Colons Semicolons and Dashes]] |
| Comma between subject and verb | "The president, announced..." | Never separate subject from verb with a comma | [[Comma Rules]] |
| Missing second comma | "My dog, a retriever ran away" | Non-essential phrases need commas on BOTH sides | [[Comma Rules]] |
| That vs. which | "The book, that I read..." | "That" = no commas, "which" = commas | [[Colons Semicolons and Dashes]] |

## Logic & Synthesis Traps

| Trap | What to Watch For | Fix | Full Topic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wrong transition direction | "She practiced hard. However, she won." | Match transition to logical relationship | [[Transitions and Logical Flow]] |
| "Sounds academic" ≠ correct | Picking "furthermore" because it sounds formal | Determine relationship first, then match | [[Transitions and Logical Flow]] |
| Concession vs. contrast confusion | Using "conversely" when "nevertheless" is needed | Contrast = opposite ideas. Concession = surprising outcome despite expectation | [[Transitions and Logical Flow]] |
| True but off-goal synthesis | Answer uses correct data but wrong purpose | Read the GOAL first, eliminate non-matching | [[Rhetorical Synthesis]] |
| Only mentions one of two items | Goal says "contrast X and Y" but answer only has X | Both items must appear | [[Rhetorical Synthesis]] |

## Mnemonics Cheat Sheet

| Rule | Mnemonic |
|---|---|
| Who vs. whom | "hi**m** → who**m**" (both end in m) |
| I vs. me | Remove the other person and listen |
| Linking verb test | Replace with "is/are" — if it works, use adjective |
| Subject-verb agreement | Cross out everything between subject and verb |
| Idiomatic prepositions | Read verb + preposition combo out loud |
| Possessive decision | Step 1: Does it own something? Step 2: Does the plural end in -s? |
| Its / it's / its' | "It's" = "it is." "Its'" = never correct. |
| Good vs. well | "Superman does **good**. You do **well**." |
| -ing vs. -ed participle | The movie is interest**ing** (cause). I am interest**ed** (receiver). |
| Dangling modifier | Read the opener → "Who is doing this?" → must be the next subject |
| Hard vs. hardly | "Work hard" (effort) ≠ "work hardly" (barely) |
| Comma splice test | Both sides complete sentences + no FANBOYS = splice |
| Colon test | Cover the right side. Can the left stand alone? If not → wrong. |
| Semicolon test | Same as a period — needs a complete sentence on BOTH sides |
| Parallelism | Check the form of item 1 in the list. All others must match. |
| Correlative conjunctions | Draw a line between the pair. Structures after each half must be twins. |
| Subjunctive | "If...would" → use "were" in the if-clause |
| Past perfect | "By the time" or "before" → earlier event gets "had + participle" |
| Gerund vs. infinitive | Intent verbs (want, plan, hope) → infinitive. Process verbs (enjoy, avoid, keep) → gerund. |
| Preposition "to" test | Can you replace "to" with "toward"? If yes → preposition → use gerund |
| "but" vs. "however" | Same meaning, different punctuation: **, but** vs. **; however,** |
| Conjunctive adverb interrupter test | Remove the word. Still one complete thought? → commas correct. Two thoughts? → need semicolon |
| Transition logic | Before reading choices: "because," "but," "for example," or "also"? |
| Rhetorical synthesis | Read the GOAL first. Ignore the data. Then match. |

## ⚡ High-Stakes 4-Point Checklist

*Run through these four checks on every hard grammar question:*

1. **Check the Boundaries** — Is it IC + IC or IC + DC? [[Sentence Boundaries and Clause Joining]]
2. **Find the Subject** — Does the modifier at the start of the sentence actually describe the noun that follows? [[Participles]]
3. **Match the Form** — Are items in a list or correlative pair identical in grammatical structure? [[Parallelism]]
4. **Is it Essential?** — If you remove the phrase between the commas/dashes, does the sentence still make sense? [[Colons Semicolons and Dashes]]

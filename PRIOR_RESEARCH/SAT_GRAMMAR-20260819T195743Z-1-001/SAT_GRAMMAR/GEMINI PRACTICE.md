4.

Modern smartphones are equipped with numerous ______ an accelerometer, a gyroscope, and a proximity sensor.

Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?

- A.
    
    sensors; including
    
- B.
    
    sensors: including
    
    Not quite
    
    Using a colon followed immediately by 'including' is redundant, as both serve to introduce a list or examples.
    
- C.
    
    sensors, including
    
    Right answer
    
    The comma correctly introduces a non-essential participial phrase ('including...') that provides specific examples of the 'sensors.'
    
- D.
    
    sensors including
    

Consider whether the list of sensors is an essential part of the sentence or extra detail.
